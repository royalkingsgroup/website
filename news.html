Connect Facebook Page for Live Feed

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com/tools/explorer
  2. Select your Facebook App → choose the Royal Kings Group page
  3. Click Generate Access Token — grant pages_read_engagement and pages_show_list
  4. For a permanent token: exchange via Token Debugger → Extend Token
  5. Paste the token below and click Connect & Load Feed

Token stored in your browser session only. For production, proxy through your server to avoid exposing the token client-side.
