Our Story

Built on Integrity,
Driven by Performance

Founded in 2013, the Group was established with a singular conviction — that businesses across Asia-Pacific deserved a partner who would operate with complete transparency, unwavering professionalism, and genuine commitment to their long-term success.

Today, with presence spanning Singapore, Hong Kong, Indonesia and Thailand, Royal Kings Group continues to redefine what integrated business support looks like across the modern Asia-Pacific landscape.

Leadership

Founder & Chairman

Mr Sirajudeen Bin Syed Mohamed founded Royal Kings Group in Singapore in 2013 with a clear and enduring vision — to build a business platform that goes beyond conventional corporate services and acts as a genuine strategic partner in every client's success.

Under his leadership, the Group has grown from a single wholesale trading entity into a fully integrated five-pillar business platform recognised across Singapore and the Asia-Pacific region, having received over 15 prestigious industry awards including the Singapore Prestige Brand Award 2023, SME500 Award 2024, Top Leading Entrepreneurs 2024/2025 and the SIRAT Global Golden Dinar Award 2026.

Mr Sirajudeen is also the CEO of Nambikai Singapore and a deeply committed advocate for community welfare, interfaith harmony and social responsibility — values embedded at the core of the Royal Kings Group's organisational culture.

"We do not simply offer services — we invest in the growth of every business we work with. That is the Royal Kings commitment."

Mr Sirajudeen Bin Syed Mohamed  ·  Founder & Chairman
